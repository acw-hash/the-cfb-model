## Question

Does Ridge’s μ tell us anything about who covers once the market line is known?

## Method (fixed before results)

\[
P(\text{side covers}) = \Phi\!\left(\beta \cdot \frac{\mu_{\text{ridge}} - \text{line\_margin}}{\sigma}\right)
\]

- \(\sigma\) = production per-game margin sigma (`sigma_m` on walk-forward; `sigma_margin` on 2026 publishes).
- \(\beta = 1\) is today’s card; \(\beta = 0\) means Ridge adds nothing to the line.
- **Line field:** CFBD `GET /lines?year=` provider **`spread`** (closing home spread).  
  `line_margin := −median_{\text{books}}(\text{spread})` so \(\mu - \text{line\_margin} = \mu + \text{spread}\), matching home-cover direction (`margin + spread > 0`).
- Fit \(\beta\) by Bernoulli MLE on **home-cover** outcomes (pushes dropped).
- 90% CI = profile likelihood (\(\chi^2_1\) 90% threshold).
