# Task 08 — EPA/WP normalization and garbage-time filter

## Built

- `src/ncaa_quant/features/epa.py` — normalize CFBD/staged plays into a clean
  per-play EPA table; WP + Connelly garbage-time flags; uniform play weighting
  interface; efficiency aggregation helpers (EPA/play, success rate,
  explosiveness, havoc; rush/pass splits).
- `src/ncaa_quant/features/__init__.py` — re-exports the Task 8 public API.
- Tests: `tests/unit/test_epa.py` (30-play hand-labeled GT fixture across 3
  games; success-rate by down; hand-computed aggregates; WP-null fallback;
  raw-JSON loader).

## Output columns

`game_key`, `offense_team`, `defense_team`, `play_type`, `down`, `distance`,
`yardline` (= yards-to-goal), `period`, `clock` (seconds left in period),
`epa`, `wp_before`, `wp_after`, `is_rush`, `is_pass`, `is_special_teams`,
`is_penalty`, `garbage_time`, plus helpers `is_havoc`, `is_success`,
`score_margin`, `gt_rule` (`wp` | `connelly_fallback`), `gt_fallback_used`.

## Decisions / ambiguities

1. **Connelly thresholds** (published FO/Connelly / SB Nation): margin *greater
   than* 28 / 24 / 21 / 16 in Q1–Q4. OT (`period >= 5`) uses the Q4 threshold.
2. **WP boundaries**: GT when `wp_before > 0.98` or `< 0.02` (strict). Equals
   0.98 / 0.02 are *not* garbage.
3. **Success rate**: 50% / 70% / 100% of `distance` on 1st / 2nd / 3rd–4th
   (Football Outsiders / Connelly). Undefined when down ∉ {1..4} or inputs null.
4. **Explosiveness**: mean EPA on successful plays (IsoPPP-style).
5. **Havoc**: play_type ∈ {Sack, Interception(+returns), Fumble Recovery
   (Opponent)/Return TD}. CFBD does not emit a separate non-sack TFL type, so
   non-sack TFLs are undercounted — flagged here, not invented.
6. **`game_key`**: uses caller-supplied `game_key` when present; else
   `str(game_id)`. Full `season:home:away:date` keys require a games/teams join
   by the caller (Task 9+).
7. **`yardline`**: mapped from `yards_to_goal` (yards from opponent goal).
8. **Weighting**: `PlayWeighting` protocol + `UniformWeighting` only; leverage
   weights are a §3.6 research option, not implemented.
9. **Dirty CFBD fields**: `down > 4`, `period` outside 1–8, `yardline` outside
   0–100 coerced to null (same policy as Task 5 ingest).
