## STEP 0 sign audit (batch 2)

Audit **passed**. Luck is not inverted. H14 was not redefined and was not
re-run as a bug fix.

Unit test: a team that won by 30 as a 7-point favorite, with EPA margin equal
to its pre-game expectation, has `luck = +23` (home or away; the next game's
`opp_luck_last` carries +23). Formula:
`luck = (margin − closing expected margin) − (GT-filtered EPA margin − Ridge
pre-game expected EPA margin)`.

On discovery-window H14 qualifiers, every opponent last-game luck value is
positive (n=872 including 2020, mean +22.0, min +15.7). Five games with every
component from the lucky team's side:

| next game | season | lucky team | last margin | last close exp. margin | last EPA margin | last expected EPA | luck | next ATS (angle side) |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 400869001 | 2016 | Vanderbilt (home) | +23.0 | +3.0 | −9.19 | −4.33 | +24.86 | +24.50 |
| 401012283 | 2018 | Vanderbilt (home) | +31.0 | +10.0 | +12.12 | +10.51 | +19.39 | −9.00 |
| 401112098 | 2019 | Kansas (home) | −5.0 | +7.0 | −12.01 | +16.78 | +16.79 | −44.00 |
| 401282069 | 2021 | Georgia Southern (away) | −32.0 | −6.5 | −27.01 | +19.46 | +20.97 | +12.00 |
| 401403891 | 2022 | Vanderbilt (home) | −20.0 | −13.5 | −13.87 | +9.69 | +17.06 | −12.50 |

Kansas 2019 is the useful check: they **lost** as a 7-point favorite (ATS
residual −12) but EPA was far worse than the pre-game expectation, so luck is
still large and positive. The H14 residual on the next game is from the angle
team (facing the lucky team), not the lucky team.
