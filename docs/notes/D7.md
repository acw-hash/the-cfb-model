### 2. POST-HOC DIAGNOSTIC — power of the season-counting criterion

*Labeled post-hoc. Does not change the stop decision.*

Clear = `b2 > 0` and two-sided bootstrap `p < 0.10` (≈ z > 1.645), as used in D6.

| season | SE(b2) | |b2| detectable at 80% | P(clear \| b2=0.211) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2019 | 0.107 | **0.299** | 0.629 |
| 2021 | 0.138 | **0.386** | 0.455 |
| 2022 | 0.185 | **0.519** | 0.306 |
| 2023 | 0.138 | **0.386** | 0.455 |
| 2024 | 0.162 | **0.455** | 0.365 |
| 2025 | 0.134 | **0.376** | 0.471 |

Every season's 80%-power MDE exceeds the joint b2=0.211 — **no single season could detect this effect at 80% power.**

Under stable true b2=0.211 and independent season clears: **P(≥3 of 6 clear) ≈ 0.554** (Poisson-binomial; mean per-season clear prob ≈ 0.447).

**Plain statement:** the season-counting criterion was *marginally* capable of passing at the p<0.10 bar (P≈0.55), but it was a weak stability screen: within-season SEs are too large for an 80%-power test of b2=0.211. The gate's miss is consistent with underpowered season tests, not with sign-flipping point estimates (all six season b2s were positive).

### 4. Week-bucket interaction (one regression)

Model (not three separate regressions):

\[
y = a + b_1\cdot\mathrm{market} + \sum_k b_{2k}\cdot\mathrm{stack}\cdot\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{bucket}=k\} + e
\]

Buckets: weeks 1–4 / 5–9 / 10+. SEs and CIs from week-block bootstrap; equality of the three \(b_{2k}\) via Wald on the bootstrap covariance.

| bucket | b2 | 95% CI (bootstrap) | n |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 1–4 | **0.274** | **[0.112, 0.392]** | 1757 |
| 5–9 | 0.134 | [0.034, 0.222] | 1634 |
| 10+ | 0.164 | [0.059, 0.255] | 1692 |

**Interaction test:** Wald χ²(2)=9.83, **p=0.007** — b2 differs across week buckets.

Early-week b2 is largest and reliably positive. Late-week b2 is *also* reliably positive in this shared-(a, b1) specification (CI does not cover zero). The D6 separate-slice pattern (early ≫ 0, late ≈ 0) partly reflected letting a and b1 re-fit per slice (late-slice b1 was ≈1.06). The formal interaction still finds a calendar gradient (early highest), but **not** the clean “late = 0” story from three independent regressions. Structural finding: week-heterogeneous stack weight, with early weeks carrying the most — not a pure late-null.
