## Decision-point schedule (v1)

Pre-registered:

| Name | Request time |
|---|---|
| `tuesday_0600_et` | Tuesday 06:00 America/New_York of the CFB week (one call/week) |
| `slot_close` | Distinct `games.start_date` − 5 minutes (one call/slot) |

DESIGN §9.8 also lists Thu/Sat 06:00 ET and T−6h / T−1h. Those are **intentionally
deferred** for budget (TASKS.md). Adding them later invalidates backtest
comparability with runs made under this schedule.

## event_time discipline

| Path | `event_time` |
|---|---|
| Live | `captured_at` at ingest |
| Historical | Envelope **returned** `timestamp` — never the request `date` |

As-of coverage tolerance: ≥10 min before 2022-09-01, ≥5 min after.

## Credit buckets

- Live floor: `odds_rate_limit_reserve` (50). Historical refuses any call that would
  leave remaining below that floor after a 30-credit debit.
- Historical ceiling: `odds_historical_credit_ceiling` (16000); refuse without `--force`.
- Credits/call: `odds_historical_credits_per_call` (30 = 10 × markets × regions).
